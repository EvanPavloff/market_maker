## Goal

Evan wants the BasicSwap desk to **accumulate BTC**, not USD. The loop is
BTC → XMR (restock) → BTC (sell XMR at a premium), scored in sats. The open
question is whether anyone buys our XMR at a premium often enough, at a good
enough price, that restocking on Kraken (KYC, Evan's call later) would net BTC.
A 4-week proof trial answers it against a fixed success bar.

## Settled already (don't re-derive)

- **Market data (14 days):** buying XMR by taking offers costs a median +1.85% on
  BasicSwap (+1.28% on Bisq/Eigen ASB), about the whole sell premium. The best
  maker buying XMR quotes +0.33%. Demand everywhere is one-way: people buy XMR at a
  premium. Cross-venue arbitrage was checked: the book is crossed in ~24% of
  10-minute windows, but the median gap is −0.74%, so it's not a strategy.
- **Kraken XMR/BTC** (US, KYC required) costs about +0.12% in book depth plus an
  assumed ~0.40% fee. Only relevant if the trial comes back OPTIMISTIC.
- **Realistic expectation Evan was given:** a $5k pot makes roughly $100–300/yr
  in the base case, from negative to ~$2k/yr across cases. The earlier "$4k/yr"
  figure was retracted as stacked optimistic assumptions.
- **Success bar** (`next_steps.md` #17), minimum 4 weeks:
  - PESSIMISTIC: < 0.5 sell fills/week, or mean margin ≤ 0 over ≥ 4 fills.
  - OPTIMISTIC: ≥ 2 fills/week AND ≥ 8 fills AND mean margin ≥ 0.8%.
  - BASE: anything between.
  - INCONCLUSIVE: sell offer live < 50% of poller cycles.
  - "Margin" = edge vs. the mid at +1h, minus Kraken depth cost, fee and transfer fees.
